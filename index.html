Our Story

Born From
The Grill

We don't flip burgers — we smash them. Every patty is hand-pressed onto a screaming-hot grill, creating a caramelized crust that you simply can't fake and can't forget.

Our 100% Australian Brisket beef is ground fresh daily. We hand-craft our own sauces, caramelize our own onions, and obsess over every detail — because shortcuts have no place in a Smashed Burger kitchen.
